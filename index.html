Track 14

Lyric Hacker II

I must have called a thousand times

To tell you I'm sorry, for everything that I've done

Grammar: Past Modal

A logical deduction about the past. She doesn't have the exact number, but based on her desperation, she assumes it was a thousand times.

Meaning Decode

A blanket apology. She isn't apologizing for one specific event, but for the entire destruction of the relationship.

Track 21: Pronunciation

Vocal Booth

Written MUST HAVE
Spoken (Native Reduction) MUSTA

Native speakers rarely pronounce "have" fully in past modals. "I must have called" sounds like "I musta called". Practice the rhythm!
